Method and data notes

  • Hyatt's filings supply revenue, net income, income tax and shareholders' equity every quarter from 2010; they do not tag total costs and expenses or operating income.
  • Peers: 13 United States hotel companies and lodging real estate investment trusts. Return on equity leaves out any peer with negative equity, where the ratio has no meaning.
  • Quarterly amounts reported only as year-to-date figures (usually the fourth quarter) are the year-to-date total minus the previous quarter's year-to-date total: exact accounting, nothing estimated.
  • Ransomware attacks: the tracker's sector address was refusing requests (one a minute allowed), so the notebook counted claims from its twelve monthly victim lists instead.
